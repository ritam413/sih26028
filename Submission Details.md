## Abstract Summary

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY AND PROBLEM LANDSCAPE Indian Railways operates one of the most extensive railway networks in the world running over 13000 passenger trains daily across 68000 route kilometers. The network faces major operational challenges in maintaining coaching train punctuality while executing safety critical infrastructure maintenance. The existing operational paradigm relies on static Working Time Tables discrete station arrival logging in the National Train Enquiry System NTES and manual heuristic dispatching via the Control Office Application COA. When unforeseen primary disruptions occur such as Temporary Speed Restrictions TSRs Civil TMS rail defects Electrical TDMS overhead equipment faults Signaling SMMS point failures or adverse weather the resulting delays propagate non linearly across dense multi track corridors. Furthermore uncoordinated single department maintenance requests result in a 35 to 40 percent maintenance block deficit as noted in Comptroller and Auditor General Report Number 22 of 2022.

MATHEMATICAL MODELING AND HYBRID PHYSICS PREDICTION ENGINE IRIS AI uses a two tier hybrid architecture combining longitudinal train dynamics with stochastic machine learning residual regression.

A. Longitudinal Train Dynamics and Kinematic Travel Time Deterministic travel time across discrete spatial segments of length d s is computed as Travel Time equals Sum of d s divided by v max plus t accel plus t decel plus Delta t TSR plus delta t dwell

Where v max is the minimum of rolling stock Maximum Permissible Speed 160 km per hour for Vande Bharat 130 km per hour for LHB Rajdhani Permanent Speed Restrictions based on track geometry and active Temporary Speed Restrictions. Train acceleration and resistance are modeled using the standard Davis formula Total Resistance equals A plus B times v plus C times v squared plus 1000 times g times sin of theta plus 0.0004 times D times Mass Where A B and C represent rolling mechanical and aerodynamic drag coefficients theta is track gradient and D is curve degree.

B. RDSO Kavach TCAS Deceleration and Adhesion Physics Braking deceleration complies with RDSO specification RDSO SPN 196 2020 governing the Kavach Train Collision Avoidance System. Emergency Braking Distance EBD is calculated as EBD equals V squared minus V target squared divided by 2 times g times mu effective plus or minus G track plus V times t reaction Where g is 9.81 meters per second squared G track is track gradient and t reaction is the 2.5 second system response and brake pipe pressure discharge lag.
